The Quality Care Standard

What We Check.
What We Stand Behind.

This isn't just a checklist — it's a promise. Every time you bring your car to Quality Auto Care, we run through our 21-point safety inspection at no charge. Here's what we look at, and why each one matters for your family.

Brake Pad Thickness

Worn brakes are the #1 safety risk we see. We measure every pad and tell you exactly how much life is left — no guessing, no scare tactics.

Safety Critical

Tire Tread & Pressure

Bald tires in the rain? That's a family safety emergency waiting to happen. We check tread depth on all four corners and set proper pressure.

Safety Critical

Fluid Levels & Condition

Low brake fluid or burnt transmission fluid can lead to costly repairs — or worse, a breakdown with your kids in the car. We catch it early.

Preventive

Steering & Suspension

Loose steering isn't just annoying — it's dangerous, especially at highway speeds with your family on board. We check every component.

Safety Critical

Battery & Charging System

A dead battery at night with kids in the car? We test your battery's health and charging output so that never happens.

Preventive

Belts & Hoses

A snapped serpentine belt means no power steering, no A/C, and an overheating engine. We inspect all belts and hoses for cracks and wear.

Preventive

Lights & Wipers

Can you see and be seen? We check headlights, brake lights, turn signals, and wiper condition. Your visibility keeps your family safe.

Safety Critical
That's 21 points total — all checked, all free, every visit.

We don't do this to find things to sell you. We do this because we believe every family deserves to know their car is safe. If something needs attention, we'll tell you honestly. If it can wait, we'll tell you that too.
